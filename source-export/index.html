Our Story

Our Craft

At Mandarin Gourmet on Santa Teresa Boulevard, we believe that great Chinese food begins with respect for tradition and an unwavering commitment to quality. Every dish that leaves our kitchen is crafted with care - from the selection of the freshest ingredients to the precise techniques passed down through generations of culinary artistry.

Our menu features beloved classics elevated to new heights. Whether it's our famous homemade pot stickers, pan-fried to golden perfection in iron skillets, or our signature Prawns with Honeyed Walnuts - each plate tells a story of flavor, patience, and passion. We take pride in generous portions that bring families together around the table, making every meal a celebration of authentic Chinese cuisine in the heart of San Jose.

From Blossom Valley to Almaden, from Oakridge to Silver Creek, our neighbors have made Mandarin Gourmet their go-to destination for Chinese food that feels like home. We are honored to serve this community and invite you to discover why over ten thousand customers rate us among the best.

Questions

Common Questions

What meals do you serve?
Mandarin Gourmet on Santa Teresa Blvd serves authentic Chinese cuisine perfect for family dinners, casual lunches, and takeout meals. Our menu features classic favorites like savory Fried Rice and Chow Mein, available with chicken, BBQ pork, beef, or shrimp. We are famous for our homemade Pot Stickers, pan-fried to a perfect crisp and served with chili paste, soy, and vinegar. Seafood lovers enjoy our Prawns with Honeyed Walnuts, a beloved Chinese-American specialty. We also offer a personal Chicken Salad for lighter dining options.

Do you offer delivery or takeout?
Yes, we offer both delivery and pickup. Order conveniently online or by phone.

Where are you located?
We are located at 5560 Santa Teresa Blvd, San Jose, CA 95123.

What areas do you serve?
Mandarin Gourmet proudly serves San Jose and surrounding neighborhoods including Blossom Valley, Almaden Valley, Oakridge, Coyote Creek, Santa Teresa, Silver Creek, Evergreen, Berryessa, Willow Glen, and Cambrian Park.
